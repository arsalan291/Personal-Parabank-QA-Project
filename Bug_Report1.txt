Bug-PB-01

Title: Bill pay allow negative numeric values leading to balance increment

Severity: P1 (Critical)

Step to Reproduce

1.Log into Parabank with valid credential.
2.And Navigate to Bill Pay section  Via the Left navigation menu
3.Fill all mandatory information
4. In the amount Field, Enter -$250
5.Select account 1234  as the payment source and Click Send Payment

Expected Result

The System should validate the input and block the submission. and display an error message: "stating that transaction amount must be greater than zero"


Actual Result:

System processes the payment successfully and shows message "Bill Payment Complete".
Balance is increased from $1000 to $1250 instead of being debited or rejected
